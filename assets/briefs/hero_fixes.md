# Brief: Hero props — review fixes (round 2)

Read `briefs/_common.md` and `briefs/codex_hero.md`. The previous artist built `blender/hero.py`
(already exports Gaslamp, Cane, Sabre, TonicVial with previews in `renders/`). Look at those 4 PNGs,
then EDIT `blender/hero.py` (keep its structure, budgets, object names) to fix the review notes below,
re-run it headless, look at the new renders, and repeat until the notes are met.

Review notes from the art director:
- **Sabre (rework)**: blade is straight and dagger-wide. Make it a real cavalry sabre: slim blade
  (~0.22 wide at the base tapering to a point), clearly curved backward along its whole 3.2 length
  (tip offset ~0.35 from the straight line), single edge with a visible fuller groove. Guard: the
  knuckle bow must be ONE continuous curved brass bar running from the crossguard down around the
  fingers to the pommel cap — the current bow is broken into disconnected segments.
- **TonicVial (rework)**: looks faceted — use more segments and smooth shading on glass and liquid.
  Liquid must fill only the lower 2/3 of the flask with a flat top surface (meniscus plane), slightly
  inset from the glass. Make the liquid a vivid alchemical green-teal so it reads as glowing.
- **Gaslamp (polish)**: shaft too thin and plain — thicken to ~0.55 diameter at base tapering to 0.4,
  add vertical fluting (8 flutes) on the lower half and 2 collar rings. Add the ladder-rest crossbar
  on BOTH sides (currently one arm). Make the lantern hood a steeper pyramid with a brass trim edge.
- **Cane (polish)**: knob is a plain ball — flatten it slightly and add an engraved band under it;
  add a thin collar where the knob meets the wood.
